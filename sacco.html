Inakuja hivi karibuni / Coming soon

Elimu+ SACCO

Tunaandaa chama cha akiba na mikopo kwa wahitimu wa Elimu+ na jamii zao, ambapo akiba ya wanachama inawekezwa kwa busara ili ikue.

We are planning a savings and credit society for Elimu+ graduates and their communities, where members' savings are invested wisely so they can grow.

Wazo letu

Our idea

Wahitimu wengi wa Elimu+ wanajua kuhesabu bei na faida zao, lakini hawana mahali salama pa kuweka akiba wala mkopo wa bei nafuu wa kukuza biashara. Pesa ikipatikana ghafla, wengi hawajui jinsi ya kuitumia vizuri. Elimu+ SACCO itaunganisha akiba, mikopo midogo ya biashara, mafunzo ya fedha na ushauri, ili pesa iende pamoja na maarifa.

Many Elimu+ graduates can work out their prices and profit, but have no safe place to save and no affordable credit to grow. When money arrives suddenly, many are unsure how to use it well. Elimu+ SACCO would bring together savings, small business loans, financial education and mentorship, so that money comes with know-how.

Tunachopanga

What we are planning

  • Akiba ya mara kwa mara ya wanachama, inayotunzwa kwa uwazi. / Regular member savings, kept transparently.
  • Mikopo midogo ya kukuza biashara kwa wahitimu, pamoja na ushauri. / Small business-growth loans for graduates, with mentorship.
  • Uwekezaji wa pamoja wa akiba kwa busara, wanachama wakiona jinsi unavyofanya kazi. / Careful pooled investment of savings, with members able to see how it works.
  • Mafunzo ya fedha ya mara kwa mara kwenye mikutano ya kila mwezi ya Elimu+. / Regular financial education at the monthly Elimu+ forums.
  • Uhusiano na Mahitaji ya Ukuaji ili msaada wa vitu na mikopo vifanye kazi pamoja. / A link to the growth needs board so that help in kind and loans work together.

Hali ya sasa, kwa uwazi

Where we are, honestly

Bado ni mpango. Hatupokei amana, michango wala ada za uanachama kwa sasa. Chama cha ushirika cha akiba na mikopo kinachopokea akiba za wanachama nchini Kenya kinadhibitiwa kisheria, na tutaanza tu baada ya usajili na idhini zinazohitajika. Hatuahidi faida, riba wala gawio lolote hadi mpango uwe tayari na kuidhinishwa.

This is still a plan. We are not accepting deposits, contributions or membership fees. A savings and credit society that takes members' savings in Kenya is regulated by law, and we will only begin once the necessary registration and approvals are in place. We promise no profit, interest or dividend until the plan is ready and approved.

Hatua zinazofuata

Next steps

  1. Kusikiliza. Tunazungumza na wahitimu na mashirika mwenyeji kuhusu wanachohitaji.
    Listening: we are talking with graduates and host organisations about what they need.
  2. Kubuni. Tunaandaa muundo, kanuni na usimamizi wa pesa, na kuthibitisha mahitaji ya kisheria na wataalamu.
    Designing: we are drafting the structure, rules and money management, and confirming legal requirements with professionals.
  3. Usajili na idhini. Tunakamilisha usajili na idhini zote kabla ya kupokea akiba yoyote.
    Registration and approvals: we complete every registration and approval before accepting any savings.
  4. Kuanza kidogo. Tunaanza na kundi dogo la wahitimu, kujifunza kabla ya kupanua.
    Starting small: we begin with a small group of graduates and learn before we grow.
