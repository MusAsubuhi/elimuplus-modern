Elimu+ Pitching Competition

An Elimu+ pitching competition is on its way, a chance for the entrepreneurs we train to stand up and present their business ideas.

What we can say for now

Pitching is already part of how Elimu+ teaches: participants explain their idea, their numbers and their next step to their peers. The competition will take that practice to a bigger stage. We are not sharing dates, entry rules or rewards yet, because they are still being planned.

Partner organisations hear first. Details will be announced to the organisations we work with, and then shared on this website. Meanwhile, you can come to the monthly Elimu+ forums, starting on 28 October 2026.
