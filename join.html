Register Your Organisation

Elimu+ works with organisations, not individuals. If your church, NGO, school or college supports people who want to start or grow a business, we can train them together.

How an organisation works with Elimu+

Individuals cannot register for Elimu+. Every cohort is arranged through an organisation that already serves the people being trained, such as a church, an NGO or community group, a school or a college. Registration is done only by the organisation, for its own cohort.

  1. You get to know us. Read about who we are, look at what we teach and see what has happened in the businesses of past participants.
  2. You reach out. Send the short registration form below. Tell us about your organisation and the people you would like us to train, whether they are your members, your students or the people you support.
  3. We talk it through. We contact you, learn about the group, and agree the format (the quick 5-Day Crush or the fuller 12-Day SEE Programme), the venue, the timing and whether you want follow-up visits at 30, 60 and 90 days. Our 2026 rates are published, and a quotation follows.
  4. We train and follow up. We train the people under your care, then, where follow-up is booked, visit their businesses until survival is confirmed.

Registering your organisation is not a payment and not a binding booking. If you are a member of a church or a student at a school and would like to be trained, please ask your leaders to get in touch with us.
