Training that doesn't stop at the door.

Most programmes train and leave. Elimu+ trains, then follows participants into their businesses at 30, 60, and 90 days — coaching, troubleshooting, and staying until survival is confirmed. The follow-through is not an add-on. It is the programme.

North star: 75% business survival rate at 90 days.

The SEE Canvas — the tool at the heart of everything

Every Elimu+ participant completes a SEE Canvas: a practical, bilingual business planning framework built on the SAME principle — Simple, Actionable, Memorable, Experiential. Twelve sections. Two pages. One business made clear.

Rahisi. Inayoweza kufanywa. Inayokumbukwa. Inayopatikana.
(Simple. Actionable. Memorable. Experiential.)

Every lesson is grounded in a real community.

Elimu+ doesn't use invented examples. Every teaching moment is built around Mwanahamisi — an omena seller in Soweto estate, Likoni. A single mother. A real entrepreneur. Her business, her customers, her suppliers, her challenges. When participants learn contribution margin, they calculate it for her stall. When they map their competition, they map hers first. This is what it means to train locally.
