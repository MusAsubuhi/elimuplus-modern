Become an Elimu+ Mentor

Walk alongside a small circle of young people or caregivers as they turn what they learned into steps taken.

Kuwa mshauri wa Elimu+.
(Become an Elimu+ mentor.)

Who we are looking for.

You do not need a title or a degree. You need to be someone a young person or a worried parent can actually talk to.

  • You listen first, and you ask better questions than you give answers.
  • You have built something, held a job, raised a family or come through a hard season, and you can say so honestly, including what went wrong.
  • You can speak with a teenager and with a caregiver without lecturing, in Swahili, English or both.
  • You can give a steady hour or two each month for several months, and you finish what you start.

What mentoring looks like.

A small circle

One mentor and up to five people from the same Elimu+ cohort, who already know each other and have learned the same things.

Short, regular sessions

You meet for about an hour, in the open or with the partner organisation present. Each person leaves with one small thing to do before the next session.

We keep improving

After every session, everyone answers a short form so we can make the next one better. You see totals only, never who said what.

How we choose mentors.

Mentors work closely with people who trusted Elimu+ with their time, so we take care. Nobody is added to a circle on the strength of a form alone.

  1. The founders read your application. Two people read it, not a system.
  2. A conversation. We talk with you about how you would handle real situations.
  3. A referee. We call someone who knows you and has seen you work with people.
  4. A briefing and an agreement. Boundaries, confidentiality and safeguarding, written down and signed. No mentor ever meets a person under 18 alone.

Applying does not guarantee a place. We will be plain with you about what we expect, and about any costs we can cover, before you start.

Tell us about yourself.

We read every application and will contact you to arrange a short conversation.

A few honest sentences is enough.

We use these details only to consider your application and to contact you and your referee. Read our privacy notice.

Thank you. Two of us will read your application and be in touch.
