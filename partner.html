How it works — KE358 Deliverance Church Likoni, CDC

Needs image: partner-ke358-venue.jpg

KE358 Deliverance Church Likoni CDC became Elimu+'s first institutional partner and has since incorporated Elimu+ into their Compassion International budget. The partnership began with a single adult cohort — 10 women, 3 intensive days, KES 153,640 invested in participant businesses. At the 30-day mark: 9 of 10 businesses active. 7 of 10 incomes growing. Three participants identified as standout case studies for further support.
