How it works: Deliverance Church International, Likoni (KE358)

Deliverance Church International, Likoni (KE358) carries out the Compassion project in Likoni and became Elimu+'s first institutional partner. It has hosted a youth cohort in 2025 and three adult cohorts in 2026, and has incorporated Elimu+ into its Compassion International budget.

Its first adult cohort was 10 women. At the 30-day visit, 9 of 10 businesses were active. At 60 days, 8 of 10 were confirmed operating, 5 women reported rising income and our field team judged 4 businesses to be clearly growing, with KES 213,190 invested across the ten businesses. Not every business is thriving: illness, bereavement and rising input prices are slowing several, and we report that to the partner as openly as the good news. Read three anonymised case studies →

Questions partners ask

How long is the programme?

You choose. The 5-Day Crush is the quick programme: at least five 2-hour sessions per participant, with the last two days spent on costs and income. The 12-Day SEE Programme carries the same curriculum with extra time for knowledge checks, peer teaching, gallery walks and closing pitches. Either suits adults or youth. See Our Programme.

What does it cost?

For the 5-Day Crush, KES 300, 250 or 200 per participant per session depending on group size, with a minimum of five sessions. Follow-up is KES 1,000 per participant. The full table is above. These rates apply to 2026 only.

Is follow-up free?

No. Follow-up visits at 30, 60 and 90 days are priced separately, so each one is properly planned and reported. We would rather price follow-up honestly than promise it for nothing and deliver it thinly.

Who is the programme for?

Women and adults who run or want to start a small business, and youth aged 15 to 24. We work through institutions such as churches, CDCs, NGOs and colleges that already know their community.

What language is it taught in?

Swahili first, with English alongside. The materials are bilingual.

How big can a group be?

We work with groups from a handful of participants to 40 or more. For groups well over 50, we prepare a custom quotation.

What do we receive at the end?

Completed SEE Canvases, certificates of completion and a formal programme report. Follow-up visits, where booked, add 30, 60 and 90-day findings.

How do we pay?

You receive a written quotation first. We invoice from the confirmed attendance register after training. We will send payment details with the invoice.

Can we run it for our youth group or school?

Yes. The 5-Day Crush or the 12-Day SEE Programme can be run for a youth group or school; the 12-Day SEE Programme is quoted separately. Tell us the group size and timing in the form below.
