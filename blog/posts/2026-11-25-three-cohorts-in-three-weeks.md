November was our busiest month so far. In three weeks we ran three 5-Day Crush cohorts, two of them back to back at the same venue.

## Mtongwe: our first cohort outside Likoni (4 to 10 November)

Thirteen caregivers at KE740 GNCA Mtongwe took the programme in Swahili. All thirteen completed, each leaving with a SEE Canvas for their own business: who they serve, what it costs and how it earns. It was our first cohort with a new partner, and the first time the programme ran outside Deliverance Church International, Likoni (KE358). Their 30, 60 and 90-day follow-up visits are now under way.

## Youth cohort 2, "Maendeleo" (11 to 17 November)

Fifteen young people spent five days at Deliverance Church Likoni, taught in English, turning a skill or an idea into a SEE Canvas. All fifteen completed and received their certificates on 20 November.

## Youth cohort 3, "Amka Kijana" (18 to 24 November)

A second group of fifteen young people followed straight after, in the same place and the same way. Fifteen were enrolled; we will report completion once the register is closed.

## What it adds up to

Since 2025 we have now run seven cohorts and trained about 85 people, six of the cohorts at KE358. The portal and this website now count from the same place, so these numbers will not drift apart again.

## What comes next

The next forum is in early December 2026; the date will go to partner organisations and past participants first. Our next adult cohort is planned for January 2027, with dates still to be confirmed.
