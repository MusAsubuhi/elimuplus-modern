------------------------------------------------------------
- Real photos only — no stock imagery, nothing staged or studio-lit.
- No images of children in poverty — this is a business training
  programme, not a welfare organisation.
- Compress before upload. Target well under 200KB per photo where
  possible so mobile pages stay fast on Android data connections.
- Hero images: wide/landscape, at least 1600px wide.
- In-page photos (img-placeholder boxes): at least 900px on the long edge.
