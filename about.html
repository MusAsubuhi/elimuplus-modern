About Elimu+

A growing organisation building sustainable businesses in coastal Kenya — one cohort at a time.

Needs image: about-story.jpg

Why we exist.

Elimu+ was born from a simple observation: training programmes in this community were leaving too early. Participants would complete a course, receive a certificate, and return to exactly the same conditions — because no one stayed to help them navigate the first 90 days. Musa Asubuhi and James Kimani founded Elimu+ to fix this. Not by building another training programme, but by building one that doesn't stop at the door. The SEE Canvas. The SAME framework. The 30/60/90-day follow-up. These are not features. They are the reason businesses in Likoni are still running three months after training that no one expected them to survive.

Tunaanza, tunaendelea, tunafuatilia.
(We start, we continue, we follow through.)
